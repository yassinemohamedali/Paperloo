# ENTERPRISE DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM (DPA)
## Legally Binding Agreement Governing Data Processing, EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), and Trans-Border Data Transfers
### Document ID: `LEG-DPA-2026-v5.0` | Statutory Scope: GDPR (EU/UK), CCPA/CPRA, APPs, PIPEDA, Law 25 | Entity: Paperloo Infrastructure Ltd.

---

## 1. PREAMBLE & APPLICABILITY

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") supplements the Master Services Agreement ("MSA") entered into by and between **Paperloo Infrastructure Ltd.** ("Processor" / "Paperloo") and the enterprise contracting party ("Controller" / "Customer"). This DPA governs the processing of Personal Data in connection with Customer’s use of Paperloo's automated compliance auditing and script injection services.

---

## 2. PROCESSING INSTRUCTIONS & STATUTORY ROLES

1. **Role of the Parties:** Customer acts as the Data Controller (or Processor on behalf of third parties), and Paperloo acts as the Data Processor (or Subprocessor).
2. **Scope of Processing:** Paperloo shall process Personal Data solely for the purpose of executing automated website compliance crawling, synthesizing legal policies, delivering consent management scripts (`/api/paperloo.js`), and fulfilling Customer's explicit instructions.
3. **Categories of Data Processed:** Anonymized IP addresses, User-Agent header strings, visitor consent state selections, website domain HTML metadata, and authorized user account emails.

---

## 3. SECURITY MEASURES & GDPR ARTICLE 32 OBLIGATIONS

Paperloo implements technical and organizational security measures to protect Customer Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorized disclosure:

- **Encryption Standards:** Mandatory AES-256-GCM encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
- **Confidentiality Obligations:** Paperloo personnel authorized to process Personal Data are bound by enforceable non-disclosure agreements.
- **Incident Notification:** Paperloo shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within **36 hours**, after becoming aware of any confirmed Personal Data breach.

---

## 4. CROSS-BORDER DATA TRANSFERS & EU SCCS

For data transfers originating in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland to countries lacking an adequacy decision:

- **Incorporation of EU SCCs:** The parties hereby incorporate Module 2 (Controller-to-Processor) and Module 3 (Processor-to-Processor) of the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914).
- **UK Transfer Addendum:** Trans-border data transfers originating in the UK incorporate the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) International Data Transfer Addendum.

---

## 5. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS & AUDIT CLAUSES

- **Assistance with Data Subject Requests (DSRs):** Paperloo shall provide Customer with technical tools to fulfill Data Subject requests for access, rectification, erasure, and data portability within 5 business days.
- **Customer Audit Rights:** Upon 30 days' advance written notice, Customer or its designated independent auditor may inspect Paperloo's SOC 2 Type II audit reports and ISO 27001 certificates to verify DPA compliance.

---

_IN WITNESS WHEREOF, THIS DPA IS INCORPORATED BY REFERENCE INTO ALL PAPERLOO ENTERPRISE CONTRACTS._
