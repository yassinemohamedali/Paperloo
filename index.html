Paperloo: Enterprise SaaS Governance and Automated Compliance Infrastructure

Paperloo provides the underlying legal architecture, automated statutory disclosures, real-time cookie consent shields, and continuous compliance monitoring for digital agencies and modern SaaS platforms.

Continuous SaaS Governance & Multi-Jurisdictional Protection

Protect your software properties across global privacy regulations including GDPR in Europe, CCPA/CPRA in California, PIPEDA in Canada, and the Australian Privacy Principles. When statutes change, our legal synthesis engine updates live disclosures dynamically across all connected domains.

Automated Legal Disclosures & Real-Time Cookie Shield

Generate, version, and publish attorney-vetted Privacy Policies, Terms of Service, End User License Agreements (EULA), and Data Processing Agreements (DPA) tailored directly to your technical data collection patterns and third-party vendors.

Programmatic Access for Developers & Autonomous Agents

Seamlessly integrate compliance checks into continuous deployment pipelines, developer tools, and AI agent workflows via our public REST API, OpenAPI 3.1 specification, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) server.
