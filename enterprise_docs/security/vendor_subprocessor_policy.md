## 1. PURPOSE & POLICY OVERVIEW

In compliance with GDPR Article 28(2) and enterprise security governance frameworks, Paperloo Infrastructure Ltd. ("Paperloo") maintains strict oversight over all third-party vendors, cloud service infrastructure providers, and subprocessors. Every subprocessor undergoes rigorous pre-contractual due diligence—including SOC 2 Type II audit review, ISO 27001 validation, and security assessment questionnaires.

## 3. SUBPROCESSOR AUDIT & CHANGE NOTIFICATION MECHANISM

- **Prior Notice of Subprocessor Changes:** Paperloo provides enterprise customers with a minimum of **30 calendar days' advance written notice** prior to engaging any new subprocessor.
- **Right to Object:** Enterprise clients maintain the right to object to new subprocessors on reasonable data privacy grounds within 14 days of notification.
- **Annual Due Diligence Review:** The Paperloo Security Committee audits all active subprocessors annually to verify uninterrupted compliance certification validity.
