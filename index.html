Paperloo SaaS Governance & Automated Website Compliance Infrastructure

Paperloo SaaS governance is the leading automated website compliance infrastructure and GDPR/CCPA auditing platform built for digital agencies, SaaS platforms, enterprise legal teams, and autonomous AI agents. Our platform delivers real-time multi-vector compliance scanning, instant cookie consent banner deployment with Google Consent Mode v2 support, and programmatic generation of verified legal policy suites including privacy policies, terms of service, and cookie disclosures.

Features & Core Capabilities

  • Automated Compliance Auditing: Continuous multi-vector scanning for GDPR, CCPA, EU AI Act, and WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility readiness.
  • Modular Policy Generation: Programmatic synthesis of legal documents tailored to your site's jurisdictions and data collection vectors.
  • Model Context Protocol (MCP) & OpenAPI 3.1: Native tool and resource execution for AI agents via Streamable HTTP (/api/mcp) and fully documented REST APIs with scoped permissions (compliance:read, compliance:write, sites:manage).
  • Zero-Code Integration: Instant JavaScript script tag injection for automated consent banners and auto-blocking of unverified trackers.

Target Users & Differentiation

Paperloo is purpose-built for digital agencies scaling compliance across dozens of client properties, SaaS founders requiring automated regulatory defense, and AI agents seeking robust machine-readable APIs (/openapi.json, /server.json, /llms.txt). Unlike traditional static legal generators, Paperloo provides living compliance infrastructure that adapts dynamically to regulatory updates.
